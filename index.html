What this computes. Blood-flow traces are de-spiked, resampled to 100 samples/min and normalised to the baseline window. With SFDI data it also fits the Brownian diffusion coefficient (Db) to the measured speckle contrast and derives absolute and relative CMRO₂.

Verified against MATLAB. The maths here is a direct port of the lab's MATLAB pipeline, checked against MATLAB reference output — Db matches bit-for-bit, every other output to better than 1 part in 1012. Open test.html to run those checks in your own browser.

Reads MATLAB v6, v7 and v7.3 files. Works offline once loaded.
